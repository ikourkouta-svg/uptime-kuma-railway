# The Unattended Portfolio

Strategy memo — prepared for J. Kourkoutas · Athens / Lusaka · 6 September 2026

Eight ways to convert existing assets (Sub-Saharan market access, 100+ closed export
projects, a working Clay/Instantly/HeyReach outbound stack) into businesses that run
with minimal owner involvement.

Interactive version: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f0c5d330-9388-4c58-9fd0-bdbcc363b268

## 1. The constraint

Every business has three labour layers. AI collapses one, worsens the second, and
does nothing to the third.

| Layer | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Delivery** | Solved | Research, drafting, enrichment, matching, monitoring, report production. |
| **Distribution** | The real bottleneck | AI made this *worse*: cold-email reply rates fell from 5.1% (2024) to 3.43% (2026) as AI SDRs pushed 4–7× more volume into the same inboxes. |
| **Trust & liability** | Non-transferable | Signing a compliance dossier, vouching for a distributor, taking the call when a container is stuck. |

The automation-agency model dies because a well-built automation becomes invisible —
it runs cleanly for three months, the client stops noticing, and the retainer looks
like a line item without a job. ~95% of corporate GenAI pilots never reach production,
so the buyer has usually been burned once already.

Only three species survive an absent owner: **compelled** revenue (a regulator forces
the purchase), **owned** revenue (a dataset or audience you control), or **acquired**
revenue (someone else built the distribution and you bought it).

## 2. Screening criteria

1. **Compelled demand** — a deadline, customs authority, bank or tender rule forces the buy.
2. **Borrowed distribution** — Chamber, expo lists, consortium, existing clients. Not cold email.
3. **Proprietary input** — your own data. Licensing someone else's destroys margin and moat.
4. **Exception-queue delivery** — machine handles ≥95%, rest clears in 15 min/day.
5. **Bounded liability** — a wrong output costs a week, not a shipment or a licence.

## 3. The eight ventures

Ranked. Autonomy scored 3–10 against the exception-queue test.

### Tier one — compelled demand (build these)

**01 · EU Compliance Desk for African agri-exports** — Autonomy 8/10 · capex €6–12k · ~6 weeks to invoice
EUDR binds large operators from 30 Dec 2026 and SMEs from 30 Jun 2027: farm-level
geolocation, risk assessment, Due Diligence Statement in TRACES NT, fines up to 4% of
EU turnover. CBAM entered its definitive phase 1 Jan 2026 (50t threshold, authorised
declarant status, certificate surrender). Incumbent platforms cost €5,000–25,000/yr
against a realistic €15–40/tonne cost-to-serve in cooperative chains, €40–80 with
intermediaries — unaffordable for African producers. Sell €150–400 dossiers and
€1,200–4,800/yr coverage. Pipeline: WhatsApp intake → Sentinel-2/GFW deforestation
check against the 31 Dec 2020 cut-off → Claude-generated DDS package → amber plots to
exception queue. Break-even ~35 dossiers/yr.
*Kill criteria: a further Commission simplification removing third-country DDS obligations.*

**02 · Africa Trade Signal (DaaS)** — Autonomy 7/10 · capex €8–15k · ~10 weeks
Incumbents sell customs records; nobody sells verified answers to "who actually
distributes this category in Lusaka/Nairobi/Abidjan, are they solvent, who decides".
Trade intelligence heads to ~$12.5bn by 2030 (8.7% CAGR) and is winner-takes-most.
Seed from the 100-project archive. Escape the DaaS margin trap (verification doesn't
scale) via pay-per-verified-record WhatsApp verifiers in Zambia. €99 / €390 / €1,500+
per month. Target 120 subs ≈ €25–30k MRR at 18 months.
*Kill criteria: cost per verified record above €0.80 at 5,000 records.*

**03 · Tender & Grant Radar** — Autonomy 9/10 · capex €2–4k · ~4 weeks
TED, AfDB, World Bank STEP, UNGM and national portals, filtered for the EU-supplier /
African-contract corridor. Generalists exist (Euro-Bidwatch, TENDERTRACK, Jorpex);
none owns this corridor. Differentiator is the machine-written winnability assessment,
not the scrape. €79–249/mo per seat plus €300–900 bid-readiness packs (where the real
revenue is). Break-even ~15 subscribers. Doubles as the content engine for 05 and a
data source for 02.
*Kill criteria: no pack revenue or attachment to 02 within six months — fold it in.*

**04 · The Dossier Factory** — Autonomy 8/10 · capex €1–3k · ~3 weeks
The market-entry methodology as a fixed-price document instead of calendar time.
Three SKUs: Scan €490, Entry Dossier €1,200, Partner Shortlist €1,900. 48-hour
delivery, >90% gross margin, ~20 min review per unit. Converts the least-strategic
third of the consulting pipeline and acts as the tripwire offer under everything else.
*Kill criteria: review time above 45 min per dossier after the tenth unit.*

### Tier two — engines and licences

**05 · The Africa Trade Desk (weekly brief)** — Autonomy 6/10 · capex €3–6k · revenue immaterial for 12 months
Not a business — the distribution layer that makes the other seven work. B2B tech
lists command $100–150 CPM direct, and the market is drifting CPM→CPA, but the real
return is CAC reduction across the portfolio. Machine-assembled from the 03 pipeline;
one human paragraph per issue, non-negotiable. One hour every Tuesday for 18 months.
*Kill criteria: if you can't commit the hour, don't start — a stalled newsletter costs positioning.*

**06 · Lusaka WhatsApp Desk — licensed, not operated** — Autonomy 5/10 · capex €4–8k · ~8 weeks
Market is real (78% of SSA SMEs use WhatsApp as primary sales channel; $33.7bn social
commerce in 2026; $1.4tn mobile money in 2025; median six-week payback). But it's the
most crowded corner, Meta shipped a free Business Agent in June 2026, and it's
fundamentally a *sales* business. So licence it: you own platform, templates and
brand; a Lusaka operator sells and supports; you take 25–35% or €40–80/client/month
and appear quarterly. Benchmarks: $8–25k scoped builds, $1.5–4k/mo retainers.
Mandatory anti-churn component: auto-generated monthly value reports per client.
*Kill criteria: no named operator with a Zambian SME sales record — then it's you moving to Lusaka.*

### Tier three — capital, not effort

**07 · Buy the distribution** — Autonomy 9/10 · capex €40–150k · ~12 weeks to cashflow
Skips Layer 2 entirely. Closed-transaction data: median profit multiples ~2.0×,
revenue 2.0–4.0×, e-commerce SDE 2.5–4×, SaaS 4–10× ARR. The 2026 buyer screen —
>30% margins, recurring revenue, **under 15 owner-hours/week** — is exactly the target
profile, and such listings are explicitly labelled. Buy trade/logistics/customs-adjacent
assets, install the exception-queue architecture, point them at the EU–Africa corridor.
Lean Six Sigma background is the operative skill: most small acquisitions fail on
operational transfer, not price.
*Kill criteria: >40% revenue in one customer, or no 12 months of platform-verified figures.*

**08 · Access royalty vehicle** — Autonomy 4/10 · time-only capex · ~4 months
Success fee plus 1–3% of territory revenue for three years instead of a market-entry
fee. AfCFTA intra-African trade is ~$220bn with rules of origin concluded (July 2026).
Highest ceiling, lowest autonomy — royalty deals need negotiation, monitoring and
enforcement, all Layer 3. Treat as a long-dated call option funded by 01–04. Royalty
on gross revenue in named territories, minimum annual payment, 3–5 positions maximum.
*Kill criteria: any counterparty refusing audit rights and a minimum payment.*

## 4. Shared infrastructure

One architecture, eight businesses — this is what makes running four simultaneously
realistic.

- **Orchestration** — self-hosted n8n on the existing Railway setup; cron-driven, versioned, shared run log.
- **State** — Supabase/Postgres as single record of truth. One contacts table, one companies table, one events table. This is what lets 03 feed 02 feed 05.
- **Judgement** — Claude API for classification, scoring, drafting, dossier generation. Prompts versioned in git; every output carries a confidence score that drives routing.
- **Exception queue** — one Slack channel or Notion view. 15 min/day clears it. If it grows faster than you clear it, the threshold is wrong, not the model.
- **Money** — Stripe subscriptions with dunning; annual billing at −20% for anything regulatory.
- **Anti-invisibility** — automated monthly value reports to every client. Highest-ROI component in the stack; the only defence against churn-by-forgetting.

## 5. First ninety days

Under €15k capex, roughly one focused day per week. Each step gates the next.

1. **Days 1–10 — Validate compelled demand before writing code.** Twenty calls: ten African exporters/cooperatives in coffee, cocoa or timber; ten EU importers from the existing book. One question: what is your EUDR plan for December, and what have you budgeted. Fewer than six with no plan and a budget → stop, go to 03.
2. **Days 8–25 — Ship the Tender Radar as the cashflow floor.** Cheapest build, fastest revenue, doubles as content engine. Fifteen subscribers covers infrastructure for everything else.
3. **Days 20–45 — Build the Compliance Desk against three *paid* pilots.** Free pilots teach nothing about willingness to pay. Target: first DDS end-to-end in under 30 min of your time.
4. **Days 30–50 — Launch the Dossier Factory off the same data layer.** Fastest revenue per hour invested; funds the rest.
5. **Day 45 — Start the weekly brief. One hour every Tuesday.** The list takes 18 months to matter, so the only available mistake is starting late.
6. **Days 60–90 — Instrument, then decide.** Measure three numbers only: exception-queue minutes/day, cost per verified record, revenue per hour of your time by product. Kill the worst performer. Only then open the acquisition search or the Lusaka licence.

## 6. What to refuse

- **A general AI automation agency** — crowded, retainers with no defined deliverable, revenue scales with your closing hours.
- **Another cold-outbound lead-gen offer** — volume up 4–7×, reply rates down to 3.43%, deliverability tightening annually. Point the stack at your own products.
- **Anything requiring authority you don't hold** — legal compliance, customs classification or emissions verification signed in your own name is unbounded liability. Partner with a licensed verifier, take the origination margin.
- **Four bespoke builds instead of one shared stack** — that is four jobs, and you will end up operating whichever one shouts loudest.
